🍔 Juicy Homemade Beef Burgers 🍔

The ultimate homemade burger recipe!


About This Recipe

I present to you the ultimate beef burger recipe that’s wholesome, customisable and will rival your local takeaway! Easy to whip up, you can make this on the stovetop or the barbecue grill.

⭐ Serves: 4 people    |    Difficulty: Easy


Ingredients
Ingredient Amount
Minced (ground) beef 500 g (1 lb)
Panko breadcrumbs 30 g (½ cup)
Egg 1
Worcestershire sauce 1 tbsp
Ketchup 1 tbsp
Sea salt flakes 1 tsp
Freshly cracked black pepper ½ tsp
Olive oil 2 tbsp
Water 2 tbsp (optional)

🥬 TO SERVE — Click to expand
  • 🍞 4 brioche rolls, halved and toasted
  • 🧀 4 slices of gouda cheese or burger cheese
  • 🍅 2 tomatoes, finely sliced
  • 🧅 1 red onion, finely sliced 😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭😭
  • 🥬 Little gem lettuce leaves, picked and washed
  • 🥒 2 tbsp sliced gherkins (dill pickles)
  • 🥄 4 teaspoons whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 🍅 4 teaspoons ketchup
  • 🌭 4 teaspoons American mustard

👨‍🍳 Cooking Tips
💡 Tip: Don't overwork the beef mixture when forming your patties. This helps keep your burgers juicy and tender!
🔥 You can cook the burgers on a stovetop or on a barbecue grill.
