The Little Bird

Benny looked behind a bush.
There was a little yellow bird sitting on the ground.
One of its wings was hurt.
“I’m trying to get home,” the bird said sadly, “but I can’t fly.”
Benny looked toward the tall hill.
He really wanted to reach his star.
But he knew what he had to do.
“Don’t worry,” Benny said. “I’ll help you.”
