A Wonderful Surprise

Suddenly, Benny heard a familiar sound.
“Chirp! Chirp!”>
The little yellow bird flew over the hill.
“You made it!” she said.
Benny smiled.
Then the bird dropped something into his paws.
It was a tiny glowing flower.
“This flower only opens under the stars,” she explained.
Benny looked at the glowing flower and then at the sky.
His heart felt warm.
He realized that helping someone had given him something much more precious than a star.
