The Brightest Light

Benny returned home the next morning.
He placed the golden feather and glowing flower beside his bed.
From that day on, Benny and the little bird became best friends.
Every night, they sat together and watched the stars.
Benny never forgot his wish.
But he learned something even better:
You don't have to reach the stars to make the world brighter.
Sometimes, a small act of kindness can make someone’s whole world shine.
And Benny’s little heart shone brighter than any star in the sky.
The End. ⭐
