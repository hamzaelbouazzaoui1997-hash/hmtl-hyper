The Star

At last, Benny reached the top of the hill.
The sky was turning dark blue.
Benny looked up.
There it was!
His little star was shining brightly above him.
“It’s even more beautiful from here,” Benny whispered.
Benny reached up as high as he could.
But the star was still far, far away.
He laughed.
“I guess some things are meant to be admired, not held.”
