A New Adventure

Benny returned home with the little bird beside him.
As they watched the stars, Benny noticed something strange.
The tiny star he had wished to touch was glowing brighter than ever!
Suddenly, a little trail of sparkling light appeared across the sky.
“Where do you think it leads?” asked the bird.
Benny smiled.
“I think it’s the beginning of another adventure!”
The two friends packed their bags and followed the sparkling trail into the forest.
They didn't know where it would take them, but they knew they would explore it together.
And so, Benny’s next adventure began...
The End. ⭐🐰
