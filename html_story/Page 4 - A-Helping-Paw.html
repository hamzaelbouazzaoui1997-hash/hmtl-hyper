A Helping Paw

Benny carefully carried the little bird through the forest.
They stopped beside a tall oak tree.
“Is this your home?” Benny asked.
“Yes!” chirped the bird happily.
Soon, the bird’s family flew down from their nest.
“Thank you!” they said.
The little bird gave Benny a tiny golden feather.
“Please take this with you,” she said. “It will remind you of our friendship.”
Benny smiled and continued his journey.
