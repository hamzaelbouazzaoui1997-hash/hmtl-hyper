The Big Hill

Benny hopped and hopped until he reached the bottom of the tallest hill.
It was much steeper than he expected.
“Oh dear,” Benny said.
He started climbing.
Halfway up, Benny became tired.
His paws hurt, and his backpack felt heavy.
“I’ll never make it,” he sighed.
Then he remembered the little bird.
Benny took a deep breath.
“I can keep going!”
