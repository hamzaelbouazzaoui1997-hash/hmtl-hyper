Benny’s Wish

Benny was a little rabbit who lived in a cozy burrow beside a beautiful green forest.
Every evening, Benny sat outside his home and looked at the sky.
One night, he noticed one tiny star shining brighter than all the others.
“I wish I could touch that star,” Benny whispered.
And that night, Benny made a very special plan.
