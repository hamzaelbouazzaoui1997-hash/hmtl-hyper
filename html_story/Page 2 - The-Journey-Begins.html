The Journey Begins

The next morning, Benny packed his little blue backpack.
Inside were three carrots, a bottle of water, and his favorite red scarf.
“I’m going to climb the tallest hill in the forest!” he said.
Benny hopped along the forest path.
He crossed a little stream and walked under tall trees.
Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice.
“Help! Is anybody there?”
